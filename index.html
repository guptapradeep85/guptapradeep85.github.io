Professional Summary

Computer Scientist with a Ph.D. in Computer Science & Engineering and 17+ years of academic, research, and administrative experience. My work centers on Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, Deep Learning, Federated Learning, and IoT-enabled systems.

I have developed advanced hybrid models integrating Vision Transformers, CNNs, GoogLeNet, and GANs for applications in smart agriculture and healthcare. My research has been published in SCIE-indexed journals, Scopus-indexed journals, book chapters, and international conference proceedings by reputed venues such as Springer and JoVE.

Beyond research, I bring demonstrated academic leadership through roles in NBA coordination, NAAC support, ISO audit processes, departmental administration, conference organization, and student research mentoring.
