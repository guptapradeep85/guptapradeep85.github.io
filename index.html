Professional Summary

Computer Scientist Working Across Trustworthy AI, Vision, Agriculture, and Healthcare

Computer Scientist with a Ph.D. in Computer Science & Engineering and 17+ years of academic, research, and administrative experience. His expertise spans Artificial Intelligence, Computer Vision, Deep Learning, Federated Learning, and IoT-enabled systems.

His research develops hybrid models integrating Vision Transformers, CNNs, GoogLeNet, and GANs for applications in smart agriculture and healthcare, with publications in SCIE-indexed journals, Scopus-indexed journals, book chapters, and international conference proceedings.

He combines research productivity with institutional leadership through NBA coordination, NAAC support, ISO audit processes, departmental administration, conference organization, and student research mentoring.
